**Spotifast Spicy Lyrics** 0.14.2-spicy.1 is an unofficial fork of [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) 0.14.2. It adds the changes of Spotifast 0.14.2 to the fork. The word-by-word karaoke lyrics, the romaji for Japanese songs and the Spicy Lyrics setup menu stay the same.

![The full screen lyrics view fills the active line word by word](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics/main/docs/spicy/demo.gif)

## Changed in Spotifast 0.14.2

- **The first sign-in needs your own Spotify app.** The shared Spotify app does not accept new sign-ins now. Make a free app in the Spotify developer dashboard, then paste its Client ID in the sign-in screen. The screen shows each step. If you signed in before, the app continues to work. [Getting started](https://spotifast.rocks/getting-started/#make-a-spotify-app) has the steps.

## New in Spotifast 0.14.2

- **Local files play at all sample rates,** also 48 kHz, hi-res and mono files.
- **You can add local files to the queue.**
- **Local files show their own cover and lyrics.** The lyrics come from a `.lrc` file next to the song or from the tags of the song.
- **A help page explains the common errors,** for example "Spotify refused the audio key".

## Fixed in Spotifast 0.14.2

- **Fewer "Spotify refused the audio key" errors.** The app asks for one audio key at a time and waits after a refusal.
- **A banner explains a refused audio key.** Another banner tells you when Spotify makes the shared app slow.
- **A playlist with hidden songs does not crash the app** when you scroll to its end.
- **A `.lrc` file with a byte order mark keeps its first line.**
- **Full screen lyrics use the full width of the window.**
- **Repeat One changes to Repeat All when you go to a different song,** as in the Spotify apps.
- Other fixes for the remote volume, the keyboard shortcuts, the dates, the Winamp mini player and Your Top Songs.

Read the [Spotifast 0.14.2 release notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.14.2) for more information.

## Spicy Lyrics features

These features did not change in this release. They work together with the new local file lyrics.

- **Word-by-word karaoke lyrics** in the side panel, the full screen view and the cinema view.
- **Spicy Lyrics as the first lyrics source,** with your own free key. Spotify and LRCLIB stay as the fallback. For a local file, the lyrics of the file come first.
- **The fill works for all synced lyrics.** Songs with line timing only also fill word by word.
- **Romaji for Japanese songs,** from Spicy Lyrics or from a built-in dictionary. The dictionary also gives romaji for the lyrics of local files.
- **The Spicy Lyrics setup menu,** from the lyrics header or from Settings → Spicy Lyrics.
- **Updates from this fork only.** The releases have the signature of the fork key.

## Install

- **Windows:** download `…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and open it.
- **Linux:** download the `.AppImage`, the `.deb` (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04 and newer), the `.rpm` (Fedora) or the `.flatpak`.

This release replaces an installed Spotifast or Spotifast Spicy Lyrics and keeps its settings and sign-in. Read the [README](https://github.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics#readme) for the Spicy Lyrics setup, the services that the app contacts, and the build steps.
