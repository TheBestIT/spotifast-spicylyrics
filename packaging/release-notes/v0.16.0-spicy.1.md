**Spotifast Spicy Lyrics** 0.16.0-spicy.1 is an unofficial fork of [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) 0.16.0. It adds the changes of Spotifast 0.16.0 to the fork. The word-by-word karaoke lyrics, the romaji modes, the duet layout and the Spicy Lyrics setup menu stay the same.

![The full screen lyrics view fills the active line word by word](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics/main/docs/spicy/demo.gif)

## New in Spotifast 0.16.0

- **Windows Media Player skins.** The mini player can use a Windows Media Player `.wmz` skin and a Winamp skin.
- **Artist pages show the monthly listeners** and the releases that come soon, with a countdown to their date.
- **Sort the songs of an album by plays.**
- **Settings shows the size of your stored data.** You can clear the cached audio there.
- **Copy the details of a song** from a new submenu in the song menu.
- **The links in a playlist description open when you click them.**
- **The changes from other devices show immediately,** when Spotify sends them. The app does not wait for the next check.
- **The top bar is easier to use with a screen reader and a keyboard.** Its controls show a focus ring.

## Fixed in Spotifast 0.16.0

- **Soloist connects again after its connection stops,** and a Soloist that is still open does not block the next start.
- **The volume of Soloist follows the slider while you move it.** On Windows, the visualizers keep their level when the volume decreases.
- **The cause of a Soloist stop stays in `soloist-output.log`.** The app removes your key from that file.
- **A window that the compositor left in full screen opens as a window again.**
- **A portable copy keeps `app.ron` in its own folder.**
- **The update check and the download use one connection.**
- Many other fixes for Connect, playlists, artist pages and Home.

Read the [Spotifast 0.16.0 release notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.16.0) for more information.

## Spicy Lyrics features

These features did not change in this release.

- **Word-by-word karaoke lyrics** in the side panel, the full screen view and the cinema view.
- **Spicy Lyrics as the first lyrics source,** with your own free key. Spotify and LRCLIB stay as the fallback. For a local file, the lyrics of the file come first.
- **Four romaji modes for Japanese songs:** romaji on top, kanji on top, kanji only and romaji only.
- **Duets:** the lines of the second singer align to the right. Background vocals show as a smaller italic line.
- **The Spicy Lyrics setup menu,** from the lyrics header or from Settings → Spicy Lyrics.
- **Updates from this fork only.** The releases have the signature of the fork key.

## Install

- **Windows:** download `…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and open it.
- **Linux:** download the `.AppImage`, the `.deb` (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04 and newer), the `.rpm` (Fedora) or the `.flatpak`.

This release replaces an installed Spotifast or Spotifast Spicy Lyrics and keeps its settings and sign-in. Read the [README](https://github.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics#readme) for the Spicy Lyrics setup, the services that the app contacts, and the build steps.
