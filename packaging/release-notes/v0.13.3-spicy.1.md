The first release of **Spotifast Spicy Lyrics**, an unofficial fork of [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) 0.13.3. It adds word-by-word karaoke lyrics in the style of [Spicy Lyrics](https://spicylyrics.org), romaji for Japanese songs, and a menu to set up your Spicy Lyrics key. Everything else is the same as Spotifast 0.13.3.

![The full screen lyrics view fills the active line word by word](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics/main/docs/spicy/demo.gif)

## New

- **Word-by-word karaoke lyrics.** The line that plays lights up from left to right with a soft edge, in the side panel and in the full screen view.
- **Spicy Lyrics as the first lyrics source.** With your own free key, lyrics come from the Spicy Lyrics API, with syllable timing for many songs. Spotify and LRCLIB stay as the fallback.
- **The fill works for all synced lyrics.** Songs with line timing only also fill word by word.
- **Romaji for Japanese songs.** A Romaji button in the lyrics header shows Japanese lyrics in Latin letters. A built-in dictionary romanizes the songs that Spicy Lyrics does not.
- **Spicy Lyrics setup menu.** The sparkles button in the lyrics header, or Settings → Spicy Lyrics, opens the steps, the links and the key field. The app checks the key when you save it.
- **Updates from this fork.** The app updates from the releases of this repository only. The releases are signed with the key of the fork.

## Install

- **Windows:** download `…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and open it.
- **Linux:** download the `.AppImage`, the `.deb` (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04 and newer), the `.rpm` (Fedora) or the `.flatpak`.

This release replaces an installed Spotifast and keeps its settings and sign-in. Read the [README](https://github.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics#readme) for the Spicy Lyrics setup, the services that the app contacts, and the build steps.
