**Spotifast Spicy Lyrics** 0.17.2-spicy.1 is an unofficial fork of [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) 0.17.2. It adds the changes of Spotifast 0.17.2 to the fork. The word-by-word karaoke lyrics, the romaji modes, the duet layout and the Spicy Lyrics setup menu stay the same.

![The full screen lyrics view fills the active line word by word](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics/main/docs/spicy/demo.gif)

## New in Spotifast 0.17.2

- **Updates install themselves.** A copy that updates itself downloads the new version and installs it the next time Spotifast starts, or right away if you restart. Settings has a new Updates section with an **Update automatically** switch. Updates come from the releases of this fork only.
- **The heart opens a checklist of your playlists,** with their covers. Tick or untick Liked Songs and any playlist in one place.
- **Omarchy themes bring their own shades.** Panels, selections and muted text use the colors that the theme defines.

## Fixed in Spotifast 0.17.2

- **Soloist plays again on Windows.** The newest Soloist build of Spotify crashes at start on Windows. A new build that keeps crashing sends Spotifast back to the last build that worked.
- **A song picked on your phone plays,** not the first song of the playlist.
- **Songs start again after autoplay took over,** also with shuffle on.
- **A click on a song far down the queue plays only that song.**
- **Soloist ends with Spotifast,** also when Spotifast stops in a crash or is stopped by force.
- **Soloist music does not break up on Linux** when the computer is busy.
- **No rectangular shadow round the skins on Windows.**
- **A Sonos that you started from your phone can be controlled,** and it keeps its volume.
- **Next up does not show a song ten times,** and Recent shows each play once.
- **The sign-in screen shows the whole redirect address.**

Read the [Spotifast 0.17.2 release notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.2) for more information.

## Spicy Lyrics features

These features did not change in this release.

- **Word-by-word karaoke lyrics** in the side panel, the full screen view and the cinema view.
- **Spicy Lyrics as the first lyrics source,** with your own free key. Spotify and LRCLIB stay as the fallback. For a local file, the lyrics of the file come first.
- **Four romaji modes for Japanese songs:** romaji on top, kanji on top, kanji only and romaji only.
- **Duets:** the lines of the second singer align to the right. Background vocals show as a smaller italic line.
- **The Spicy Lyrics setup menu,** from the lyrics header or from Settings → Spicy Lyrics.
- **Updates from this fork only.** The releases have the signature of the fork key.

The full screen view of the fork keeps its own line layout and colours. Thus the active line does not become larger or change to the accent colour, as in Spotifast 0.17.0. It fills word by word in white.

## Install

- **Windows:** download `…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and open it.
- **Linux:** download the `.AppImage`, the `.deb` (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04 and newer), the `.rpm` (Fedora) or the `.flatpak`.

This release replaces an installed Spotifast or Spotifast Spicy Lyrics and keeps its settings and sign-in. Read the [README](https://github.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics#readme) for the Spicy Lyrics setup, the services that the app contacts, and the build steps.
