**Spotifast Spicy Lyrics** 0.17.1-spicy.1 is an unofficial fork of [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) 0.17.1. It adds the changes of Spotifast 0.17.0 and 0.17.1 to the fork. The word-by-word karaoke lyrics, the romaji modes, the duet layout and the Spicy Lyrics setup menu stay the same.

![The full screen lyrics view fills the active line word by word](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics/main/docs/spicy/demo.gif)

## New in Spotifast 0.17.0 and 0.17.1

- **Windows Media Player skins run their own scripts.** The scripts run in a sandbox with no access to your files, the network or other programs. Drawers, hidden panels, separate windows, playlists and equalizers work as in Windows Media Player.
- **Two new skins: Spotifast Tall and Spotifast Wide.** They show MilkDrop, an equalizer or your queue in their glass. A new install opens the mini player in Spotifast Wide. A skin that you selected before stays.
- **Settings shows a picture of each skin.** Settings also links to the Windows Media Player Skins Collection of the Internet Archive.
- **MilkDrop in the skins.** The visualization area of a Windows Media Player skin shows MilkDrop. A Winamp skin shows MilkDrop as a panel of the player.
- **MilkDrop behind the lyrics.** In the full screen lyrics view, press **V** or click the empty space to show MilkDrop behind the words. Press **N** and **P** to change the preset. Click the album art to show only MilkDrop.
- **A full screen button in the player bar,** at the right of the volume slider.
- **A new Soloist setup** with three steps, the download progress and help for the pairing.
- **Open a Spotify link from the search bar.** Paste the link and push Enter.
- **Shift+Q adds the selected songs to the queue. Shift+N plays them next.**
- **Artist pages show the compilations,** near the albums and the singles.

## Fixed in Spotifast 0.17.0 and 0.17.1

- **Spotifast asks for the Spotify lyrics correctly.** It also forgets the old "no lyrics" answers in the cache, and asks for those songs again.
- **A busy lyrics service does not show an error.** Spotifast waits and asks again.
- **When Soloist stops, Spotifast stays open.** It starts Soloist again and continues the song. Soloist keeps the volume that you set.
- **More Windows Media Player skins show correctly,** and their dials follow the mouse.
- **A shared song link selects the song** on its album.
- **A restart keeps the playlist that plays** and the correct song during autoplay.
- **Hyprland does not show a grey haze around the skins.**

Read the [Spotifast 0.17.0 release notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.0) and the [Spotifast 0.17.1 release notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.1) for more information.

## Spicy Lyrics features

These features did not change in this release.

- **Word-by-word karaoke lyrics** in the side panel, the full screen view and the cinema view.
- **Spicy Lyrics as the first lyrics source,** with your own free key. Spotify and LRCLIB stay as the fallback. For a local file, the lyrics of the file come first.
- **Four romaji modes for Japanese songs:** romaji on top, kanji on top, kanji only and romaji only.
- **Duets:** the lines of the second singer align to the right. Background vocals show as a smaller italic line.
- **The Spicy Lyrics setup menu,** from the lyrics header or from Settings → Spicy Lyrics.
- **Updates from this fork only.** The releases have the signature of the fork key.

The full screen view of the fork keeps its own line layout and colours. Thus the active line does not become larger or change to the accent colour, as in Spotifast 0.17.0. It fills word by word in white.

## Install

- **Windows:** download `…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and open it.
- **Linux:** download the `.AppImage`, the `.deb` (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04 and newer), the `.rpm` (Fedora) or the `.flatpak`.

This release replaces an installed Spotifast or Spotifast Spicy Lyrics and keeps its settings and sign-in. Read the [README](https://github.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics#readme) for the Spicy Lyrics setup, the services that the app contacts, and the build steps.
