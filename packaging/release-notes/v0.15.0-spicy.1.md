**Spotifast Spicy Lyrics** 0.15.0-spicy.1 is an unofficial fork of [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) 0.15.0. It adds the changes of Spotifast 0.15.0 to the fork. Spicy Lyrics continues to work on 0.15.0: the word-by-word karaoke lyrics, the romaji for Japanese songs and the Spicy Lyrics setup menu stay the same.

![The full screen lyrics view fills the active line word by word](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics/main/docs/spicy/demo.gif)

## New in Spotifast 0.15.0

- **You can listen through Spotify Soloist.** Soloist is the player of Spotify for use outside the Spotify app. It has support for lossless audio. The setup shows each step: download Soloist from Spotify, add your personal Soloist key and pair one time. Spotifast keeps Soloist up to date. The built-in player stays available at each step. Soloist can prevent the "Spotify refused the audio key" problem. Soloist needs Spotify Premium. Spotify supplies Soloist for Linux, Apple Silicon Macs and x64 Windows. [Soloist setup and limits](https://spotifast.rocks/soloist/).
- **The visualizers follow Soloist.** On Linux, the EQ and the visualizers work with Soloist through PipeWire. On Windows 11, the spectrum, the oscilloscope and MilkDrop follow the audio of Soloist. The EQ cannot change the sound of Soloist on Windows.
- **The Spotify features stay available with Soloist.** Use Settings → Account → Spotify access to get Made for you, the listening activity and the Spotify lyrics.
- **An album shows its full release date** when you put the mouse pointer on it.

Soloist does not show the audio format that it plays. Local files and full queue editing use the built-in player.

## Fixed in Spotifast 0.15.0

- **LRCLIB lyrics recover when LRCLIB has too much load.** The app tries a temporary error again, and then tries a search. Lyrics in the cache stay available during an outage. The app does not record a failed request as "no lyrics".
- **Liked Songs plays the full collection,** and not only the songs that the app loaded. The playing row shows correctly.
- **The names of local songs show spaces and accents correctly,** also in playlists from the cache.
- **Turkish titles keep the pixel font of the Winamp skin.**
- **The app shows the version of its download.** The release check stops a build when the tag and the app version are different.

Read the [Spotifast 0.15.0 release notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.15.0) for more information.

## Spicy Lyrics features

These features did not change in this release. They work together with the new Soloist player.

- **Word-by-word karaoke lyrics** in the side panel, the full screen view and the cinema view.
- **Spicy Lyrics as the first lyrics source,** with your own free key. Spotify and LRCLIB stay as the fallback. For a local file, the lyrics of the file come first.
- **Spicy Lyrics in the cache stay available during an outage,** as the Spotify and LRCLIB lyrics do now.
- **The fill works for all synced lyrics.** Songs with line timing only also fill word by word.
- **Romaji for Japanese songs,** from Spicy Lyrics or from a built-in dictionary.
- **The Spicy Lyrics setup menu,** from the lyrics header or from Settings → Spicy Lyrics.
- **Updates from this fork only.** The releases have the signature of the fork key.

## Install

- **Windows:** download `…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and open it.
- **Linux:** download the `.AppImage`, the `.deb` (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04 and newer), the `.rpm` (Fedora) or the `.flatpak`.

This release replaces an installed Spotifast or Spotifast Spicy Lyrics and keeps its settings and sign-in. Read the [README](https://github.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics#readme) for the Spicy Lyrics setup, the services that the app contacts, and the build steps.
