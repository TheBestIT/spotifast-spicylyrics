**Spotifast Spicy Lyrics** 0.14.0-spicy.1 is an unofficial fork of [Spotifast](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast) 0.14.0. It adds all the changes of Spotifast 0.14.0 to the fork. The word-by-word karaoke lyrics, the romaji for Japanese songs and the Spicy Lyrics setup menu stay the same.

![The full screen lyrics view fills the active line word by word](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics/main/docs/spicy/demo.gif)

## New from Spotifast 0.14.0

- **Start with your computer.** Spotifast can start at login, with its window or in the tray. Go to Settings → Playback on this computer → Start with your computer.
- **Local files.** Select a folder in Settings, and the local files in your playlists play from it. Only 44.1 kHz files play.
- **Listening activity.** The people button beside Queue and Lyrics shows what the people that you follow listen to. It needs local playback.
- **A new Home.** The top tiles show what you played last. Home also shows Your top mixes and New releases from the artists that you follow.
- **Play counts** on album pages and on the popular songs of an artist, with local playback.
- **Fast skips.** Spotifast prepares the next two songs in the queue, so Next starts at once.
- **Playback through a dropped connection.** A song continues from the data that it has while Spotifast connects again.
- **Audio output in the device picker.** Select the audio output of this computer below the devices.
- **Hidden songs** in Discover Weekly and the other playlists that Spotify makes for you.
- **Play next** in the song menus puts songs at the front of the queue.
- **Your own equalizer presets,** with the preamp, in Settings and in the mini player.
- **A light and a dark theme for Follow system.**
- **A white or black tray icon** on Linux and Windows.
- **A portable Windows copy keeps its data next to it,** in `spotifast-data` beside `spotifast.exe`.
- **A queue beside the full screen lyrics.** Press Q to show it or hide it.
- **A larger cover** when you click the cover of a page, and the heart of a liked song opens Add to playlist.
- **Release notes in Settings → About** open the release page of the version that you use.
- **Korean** is a new language.

## Fixed in Spotifast 0.14.0

- Pause on Linux stops the sound at once.
- Playback on Windows comes back after a Bluetooth headset connects.
- Fewer crackles and dropouts on a busy computer.
- The memory does not increase while Spotifast is in the tray.
- Windows keeps your sign-in when Credential Manager fails.
- Lyrics show when LRCLIB is busy. Spotifast waits and asks again.
- Many other fixes for playlists, Liked Songs, Repeat, the mini player and the window size.

Read the [Spotifast 0.14.0 release notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.14.0) for the full list.

## Changed

- **Linux audio.** Spotifast plays through PipeWire, a PulseAudio server or ALSA, in that sequence. The audio backend setting is gone.
- **Linux needs libpipewire 0.3.53 or newer.** The `.deb` and `.rpm` packages install it. A build from source needs the PipeWire headers and libclang.
- **The AppImage updates itself from this release.** The 0.13.3-spicy.1 AppImage cannot do this. Download the 0.14.0-spicy.1 AppImage one time from the release page. The AppImage name has no `v` now: `spotifast-0.14.0-spicy.1-x86_64.AppImage`.

## Spicy Lyrics features

- **Word-by-word karaoke lyrics** in the side panel, the full screen view and the cinema view.
- **Spicy Lyrics as the first lyrics source,** with your own free key. Spotify and LRCLIB stay as the fallback.
- **The fill works for all synced lyrics.** Songs with line timing only also fill word by word.
- **Romaji for Japanese songs,** from Spicy Lyrics or from a built-in dictionary.
- **The Spicy Lyrics setup menu,** from the lyrics header or from Settings → Spicy Lyrics.
- **Updates from this fork only.** The releases have the signature of the fork key.

## Install

- **Windows:** download `…-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe` and open it.
- **Linux:** download the `.AppImage`, the `.deb` (Debian 13, Ubuntu 24.04 and newer), the `.rpm` (Fedora) or the `.flatpak`.

This release replaces an installed Spotifast or Spotifast Spicy Lyrics and keeps its settings and sign-in. Read the [README](https://github.com/TheBestIT/spotifast-spicylyrics#readme) for the Spicy Lyrics setup, the services that the app contacts, and the build steps.
